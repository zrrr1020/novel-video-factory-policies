Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Novel Video Factory handles account, authorization, publishing, and content metadata when creators connect TikTok accounts and publish videos.

1. Information We Collect

When you authorize Novel Video Factory with TikTok, the app may receive basic account information such as your TikTok open ID, display name, avatar, and authorization status.

The app may process video file paths, titles, captions, hashtags, selected cover timing, publishing status, and related metadata needed to prepare or publish content.

The app may store OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens only as needed to keep your authorized publishing session working.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to identify the TikTok account you authorized, upload or publish videos you select, show publishing status, refresh authorization, troubleshoot failures, and maintain account settings inside the app.

We do not use TikTok account information to build advertising profiles or sell personal information.

3. TikTok API Permissions

Novel Video Factory may request TikTok permissions including user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.upload.

user.info.basic is used to identify the authorized TikTok account. video.publish is used to directly publish videos selected by the user. video.upload may be used to upload content as a draft when direct publishing is not used.

The app only acts after you authorize it through TikTok and choose content or publishing settings in the app.

4. Content and Local Files

Video, audio, subtitle, card image, and project files are selected by the user and remain under the user's control.

Novel Video Factory does not sell these files. Files are uploaded to TikTok only when the user chooses to publish or upload them through an authorized account.

5. Sharing of Information

We share information with TikTok only as required to authenticate the user, upload content, publish content, or check publishing status through TikTok APIs.

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, security needs, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

6. Data Retention

Authorization tokens, account identifiers, publishing settings, logs, and status records may be retained while the user keeps the account connected or while needed for troubleshooting.

Users may disconnect an account or request removal of stored authorization data. Some operational logs may remain for a limited period when needed for security, audit, or error diagnosis.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect authorization data and publishing records.

No system is completely secure, and users should protect their devices, TikTok accounts, passwords, and recovery methods.

8. Children's Privacy

Novel Video Factory is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for creators who are permitted to manage and publish content on TikTok.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to connect a TikTok account. You may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings or through account management features provided by the app.

You may request deletion of stored authorization data by contacting the app owner.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the product, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the app owner using the contact email associated with the TikTok Developer application for Novel Video Factory.
